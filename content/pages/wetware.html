doctrine

Body and mind are infrastructure. I treat them as something to program, not something I happen to have.

Everything below is a floor, not a peak. It runs whether or not the week cooperates.

body

Fighting leads. Muay Thai is the primary discipline, and strength exists to serve striking.

Underneath it sits a home floor that depends on nothing: kettlebell and bodyweight, a couple of short sessions a week, no gym, no ride, no scheduling. Anything beyond that is a bonus and never counts toward the floor. Through the day, easy reps that never go near failure.

The rule that keeps it alive: if the floor can be blocked by someone else's calendar, it isn't a floor.

fuel

A small surplus, protein first, cooked at home. Creatine and the boring basics, nothing exotic.

I track only long enough to stop lying to myself about what I actually eat, then stop tracking.

recovery

A fixed sleep window and the same wake time every day, weekends included. The window is the discipline; the hours in bed take care of themselves.

Breath work in the morning: diaphragm, CO2 tolerance, a few minutes of stillness. Judged on capacity, not on how it feels.

attention

The layer most people skip, and the one that decides whether the rest is mine.

  • I write my own answer before I ask a model anything, then use the model to attack it.
  • One study session a week runs with no AI at all. That session is the measurement of what I actually own.
  • AI audits, it does not coach. It reads logs I wrote. It does not set the plan, the mood or the week.
  • Feeds live in fixed windows and nowhere else.

What the study sessions are spent on is on the roadmap.

cadence

Twelve-week blocks. One new habit every two weeks, each with a condition that says plainly whether it took. A condition that fails repeats. One that fails twice was too big and gets split.

Between reviews the structure does not move. New ideas, new frameworks and better systems go on a list and get judged at the end of the block, never in the middle of one. Rebuilding the plan every time something interesting arrives is the failure mode this is built against.
