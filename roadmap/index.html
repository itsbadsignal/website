Zero Dayfinal project: find, root-cause and disclose a new bug, mythic

A real vendor said: confirmed.

steps

  1. Pick a target you are allowed to test
  2. Map its attack surface: top three entry points, and why
  3. Find a bug by audit, fuzzing or variant analysis; check it is new
  4. Root-cause it to the line, name the CWE and the primitive
  5. Prove impact: a reliable PoC in your own lab (70% over 20 runs)
  6. Disclose through the vendor, their CNA or MITRE, then publish
