Why this site is plain HTML on a black page, why the only JavaScript on it exists to scare you, and what will be posted here.

This is the first post on the site, so it is about the site.

I'm Miguel. I work as a security engineer, and in my own time, when I'm not couch-potatoing over a game of chess, I get curious and like to take things apart, break them, try to fix them, and get mad in the process. This is where I write that down: CTF writeups, notes from teaching myself how computers work from the bottom up, and the occasional post about how I live and what I think and do.

In an age where everyone is regurgitating AI slop, I found it important to bring some decency to it all, while sharing my silly thoughts. That's why this space was built. It's also a way for me to track my progress and take inventory of a few things.

Plain on purpose

The page you're reading is hand-written HTML with one small stylesheet. There is no framework and no build step: what reaches your browser is the file I typed. One monospace font on a black page, one muted gold accent, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no framework running in your tab.

The focus is on the information itself, not on fancy flashes and useless motions.

The one script

There is exactly one script on this site, /prank.js, and it does nothing useful.
