About

Who writes this.

I'm Miguel, a security engineer on the CSIRT team at LAYER8, based in Porto. Self-taught, no degree. I got here the long way: Selling websites, flipping burguers, doing help desk, NSOC analyst, then cloud security engineer, now security engineering focused on SOC Ops.

In my free time I take things apart, break them (sometimes without trying very hard), and then try to fix them, getting really mad along the way.

Right now I'm working toward vulnerability research. I'm building my own curriculum in the open (roadmap), so feel free to take inspiration, make suggestions, or just check on my progress.

Away from the terminal: martial arts, time outdoors with my wife and our pets, chasing peak human performance, and blasting techno in my headphones. I'm also finally learning to drive (manual, obviously).

This site is meant to be simple and to the point: plain, hand-written HTML on a black page. There's exactly one script on it, and it's a prank. Leave a tab open and see.
